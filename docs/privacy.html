Privacy policy

Raven PBX provides business phone service and the Raven Phone app. Last updated November 5, 2026.

Who we are

Raven PBX, 2806 N Speer Blvd, Denver, CO 80211. Email hello@ravenpbx.com. Phone (833) MY-RAVEN. The public site is ravenpbx.com. The phone system is pbx.5280pbx.com.

Account and phone service

When you sign up or we provision a line, we keep the company name, billing contact, email, service address, phone numbers, extensions, and the passwords needed to sign in.

The phone system stores call records, voicemail, and business text messages so the service can place calls, deliver messages, and show history to the extension that owns them.

The Raven Phone app

The Android, iOS, Windows, and Mac apps load the phone at ravenpbx.com. The app asks for the microphone and camera so you can make voice and video calls, and for Bluetooth when a headset is used.

Contacts you add in the app stay in that browser or device. We do not upload that contact list to sell it.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal information. We do not use call audio or text messages for advertising. Carriers and the phone network see the numbers required to complete a call or text.

How long, and how to ask

Account and call records stay while the account is active and as long as we need them for billing, abuse, or a legal request. Write hello@ravenpbx.com to ask for a copy or deletion of account information. We may keep records we are required to keep.
